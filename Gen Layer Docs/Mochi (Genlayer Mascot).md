## About GenLayer

[GenLayer](https://genlayer.com) is an AI-native blockchain protocol that
functions as a synthetic jurisdiction for trustless decision-making. It enables
decentralized dispute resolution and contract enforcement powered by validator
nodes running diverse AI models.

**Key Features:**
- Intelligent Contracts written in Python
- AI Consensus for subjective decisions
- Real-world data integration without oracles
- Natural language processing capabilities

Learn more:
- Website: [genlayer.com](https://genlayer.com)
- Documentation: [docs.genlayer.com](https://docs.genlayer.com)

## About Mochi

Mochi is GenLayer's official mascot - a friendly AI-powered cat character that
represents the innovative and approachable nature of the GenLayer protocol.
With cyber-enhanced features and an adorable design, Mochi embodies the fusion
of cutting-edge AI technology and user-friendly blockchain solutions.

## The Mochi Quest - Origin Story

Mochi was born from **The Mochi Quest**, a community design contest that
invited GenLayer community members to create the official face of the protocol.

### Contest Details

- **Duration**: October 29 - November 16, 2025
- **Prize Pool**: $500 USDC + 10,000 Discord XP
- **Requirements**: Front, back, and side views with optional pose illustrations
- **Submissions**: Community members submitted their creative interpretations

### Contest Winner

**kellyboom888** was selected as the winner for their outstanding design that
perfectly captured GenLayer's vision of combining cutting-edge AI technology
with an approachable, friendly character.
